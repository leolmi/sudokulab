Highlights are a set of objects that mark areas or cells on the schema. They are triggered by specific keywords at the start of each line:

// rows are identified by index or letter
row B, 8

// the primary cell, by coordinates
cell B4

// the secondary cells
cell2 D5, E7

// columns are identified by index
col 4

// areas or groups, by index (1-9)
sqr 6
